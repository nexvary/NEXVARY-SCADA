# NEXVARY SCADA — Executive Demonstration Mode

The Executive Demonstration Mode is designed for formal product briefings where the audience needs to understand the operational value of the platform quickly.

## Presentation rules

- All demonstration KPIs are labelled **SIMULATION / DEMO DATA**.
- No demonstration value is represented as a live government, utility or nuclear-plant reading.
- Nuclear pages are limited to **non-safety balance-of-plant**, grid-facing, historian, maintenance and training views.
- Reactor protection, safety-class instrumentation and control, engineered safety actuation and SCRAM are outside the general-purpose NEXVARY SCADA product.
- Real industrial writes remain disabled by default and require explicit commissioning.

## Suggested briefing flow

1. Executive View — national / multi-site value proposition.
2. Nuclear Power Plants — safe non-safety power-generation simulation.
3. HMI — live tags and quality.
4. Devices — industrial connectivity.
5. Alarms / Historian / Audit — operational governance.
6. About System — architecture and deployment model.
7. About Us — NEXVARY official contact channels.

## Demo objective

Show that NEXVARY SCADA is not a single-machine dashboard. It is an extensible industrial operations platform that can unify heterogeneous field equipment, operational data, alarms, history and accountability in one bilingual interface.
